Election Timeline

  • Notification: Official announcement of election schedule by the ECI.
  • Nominations: Candidates file their nomination papers.
  • Campaigning: Rallies, debates, and public outreach (stops 48 hours before voting).
  • Voting Day: Citizens cast votes using Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs).
  • Counting & Results: Votes are counted and the winning candidates are declared.
