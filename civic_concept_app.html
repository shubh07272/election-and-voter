The Election Cycle

Follow the steps from candidate registration to inauguration.

1

Registration & Declaration

Citizens register to vote. Candidates officially declare their intention to run and file necessary paperwork with the election commission.

2

Campaigning & Debates

Candidates share their platforms, hold rallies, air advertisements, and debate opponents to win over the public's support.

3

Election Day

Citizens go to polling stations to cast their secret ballots. Poll workers ensure the integrity of the voting process.

4

Counting & Results

Ballots are securely counted by officials. Preliminary results are announced, followed by an official certification of the vote count.
